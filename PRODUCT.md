# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), hosted on GitHub Pages from `logic1391/portfolio` (branch `main`, root).

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers filling software engineer roles. They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn or a job application, skim for roughly 30 seconds, and decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Aditya Raj. Success is a recruiter leaving with a clear picture of what he does and contacting him by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

He builds software and he tests it. At Ethara AI he runs Docker-based benchmarks that check whether LLM agents' code fixes actually pass tests (fail-to-pass, pass-to-pass analysis, regression detection); on his own time he ships full-stack apps. Most junior portfolios show only the building half.

## Operating Context

Visitors read on laptops and phones, often from a link in an application. No login, no forms; contact happens over email (raj3aditya@gmail.com), GitHub (logic1391) and LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current role: Software Engineer at Ethara AI, Gurugram, since May 2026 (the résumé PDF still says "Full-Stack Developer"; the site uses "Software Engineer" by the owner's choice).
- Education: B.Tech CSE (Cloud Computing & ML), Babu Banarasi Das University, Lucknow, 2022–2026.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual reference chosen by the owner: the "Brutalist" template by Modulify (credited in the footer and DESIGN.md).
- The hero heading and "Let's talk." use Bungee (the reference's chunky display style); all other text uses Poppins.
- The hero text animations stay exactly as they are: the rotating phrase that blurs out and in, the blinking caret, and the staggered entrance.
- Light mode is the default; dark mode via the toggle (remembered per visitor).
- Photos: `portrait.webp`, the owner's own photo with the background removed, shown in the hero portrait card.
- "Email me" opens Gmail compose addressed to raj3aditya@gmail.com.

- Certifications with links (Deloitte, GRAStech, IBM ×4); football medals (Inter-College silver, District bronze).
- No testimonials, metrics, or employer-approved numbers exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the build-and-test story; everything else supports it.
2. A recruiter should get role, company and contact within one screen.
3. Every claim traces to the résumé or a public repo README.
4. Personality shows in details, never at the cost of scanning speed.
