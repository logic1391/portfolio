# Portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers skimming ~30s. Action: email or LinkedIn. Proof: Ethara AI role, 7 projects from public repos, certifications. Constraints: hero heading + "Let's talk." keep the system font stack at their current sizes/weights/tracking; hero text animations untouched; light default; no résumé PDF.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Swiss International Style poster that a recruiter can scan like a timetable. Refuses the glass-card dev portfolio and the hero-over-card-row template.

OWN-WORLD: Paper white #ffffff ground, ink #111 type, 1px ink hairline grid, one signal blue (#0071e3) used as whole fields, never sprinkles. No glass, no blur, no rounded cards, no shadows. Body in Hanken Grotesk; headings in the pinned system grotesk at heavy weights; flush-left, ragged-right.

STORY: The visitor learns role and company in one screen, reads "Builds it. Tests it." as the claim, sees the proof in the Ethara work and a project index, then emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ruled top bar (name left, links right). Avatar row, then the pinned headline across the full measure. Below it a four-cell ruled fact strip: Role, Company, Based, Education. Primary action "Email me" (mailto) in a solid blue rectangle at the strip's end.

FORM: Swiss poster grid, candidate 5 of 7, seed:7993ab11173a94aa (degraded roll, no challengers).

Signature interaction: project index rows fill with a left-to-right blue sweep on hover/focus, text turning white. Motion grammar: the pinned hero entrance and rotating phrase; section hairlines draw in once as they arrive; flat medals sway; hover fills on actions and rows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
